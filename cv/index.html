Professional Summary

PhD student with a background in mathematics and computer science and an MSc in Artificial Intelligence. My research focuses on machine learning, computer vision and generative AI for longitudinal modeling, patient simulation and digital twins. I develop generalizable methods that extend beyond medical imaging, guided by the belief that the field should both benefit from and contribute to advances in machine learning and computer vision. I also contribute actively to open-source scientific software.
